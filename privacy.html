Privacy Policy — Personal rclone Setup

This application is for my personal use. It accesses Google Drive to list, read, upload, update, and delete files as needed to synchronize my selected folders.

Synced files are stored on my computer and in Google Drive. Authorization credentials are stored in my local rclone configuration. This website does not receive those credentials or my Drive files. Data is not sold.

Access can be revoked through my Google Account's third-party connections settings.
